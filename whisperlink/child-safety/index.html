CSAE is prohibited absolutely
01

Our position

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) absolutely. This page states our standards, as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Any use of ATLAS WhisperLink to produce, request, or share child sexual abuse material, or to sexualise or groom a child, is forbidden. There is no exception and no context in which it is tolerated.

02

How the app is built

ATLAS WhisperLink is end-to-end encrypted with no central server. We cannot see, scan, store, or remove user content — not as a policy choice at the moment of a report, but as a property of the design. We state this plainly because a standards page that implies scanning we cannot perform would mislead both users and reviewers.

03

What the app does provide

  • Pairing is mutual. No one can contact a user who has not accepted them. There is no directory, no username search, and no way to be messaged by a stranger who has not been let in.
  • Identity verification. Every new contact is held back until the two people compare a safety number, so a user knows who they are actually talking to.
  • Blocking. A blocked person cannot message, call, reach the user through a shared group, or pair again.
  • Nearby meeting is opt-in, off by default, and every meeting needs an explicit acceptance on both phones.
  • Minimum age. The app is not for children under 13, or a higher age where local law requires one.
04

Reporting

Reports go to atlaswhisperlink.support@gmail.com and are read by a person. We will respond, act on what is within our power — including removing a user's access to the app where we can — and direct reporters to the appropriate authority. If a child is in danger, contact your local police immediately.

05

Reporting to authorities

Where we receive a credible report of CSAE, we will report it to the relevant national authority (in the United States, the NCMEC CyberTipline) and cooperate with lawful requests. We are honest about our limits: we have no content to provide, because it never reaches us.
