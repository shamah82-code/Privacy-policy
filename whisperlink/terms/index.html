Terms & acceptable use
01

What this app is

ATLAS WhisperLink is a private messenger. Messages, calls, and files travel directly between the devices of people who have paired with each other, protected by end-to-end encryption. There are no accounts, no phone numbers, and no central servers storing your messages.

02

What we can and cannot see

We cannot read your messages, see who your contacts are, or recover anything for you. Two optional helper services exist and are described in the Privacy Policy: a wake-up relay that learns only that some device should check for mail, and a call relay that may carry call audio and video between phones that cannot reach each other directly. Neither can read your content.

03

Your responsibilities

You agree not to use ATLAS WhisperLink to:

  • harass, threaten, stalk, or impersonate anyone;
  • share sexual content involving minors, or content that sexualises a child in any way;
  • share content you have no right to share;
  • do anything that is illegal where you live.
04

Children

This app is not for children under 13. Where local law sets a higher age for consenting to this kind of service, that higher age applies instead.

05

Blocking is your control

Because no server of ours holds your messages or your contacts, we cannot remove a user from the network or delete anything from anyone's phone. While you have someone blocked, they cannot message you, call you, reach you in a shared group, or pair with you again. Blocking is instant on your device, and you can undo it at any time.

06

Reporting

You can reach a person at atlaswhisperlink.support@gmail.com. Reports open your own mail app — nothing is sent automatically, and no message content, diagnostics, or contact information is attached unless you choose to include it. We cannot retrieve or verify content, so what we can do is limited to advice and to fixing the app.

07

Illegal content and the authorities

If you believe a crime has been committed, contact your local law enforcement. We have no content to hand over and no ability to identify a user from our side.

08

Subscription

ATLAS WhisperLink requires a paid subscription, billed through Google Play or the App Store. When a subscription ends, the app stops sending and receiving messages. Cancellation and refunds are handled by the store, under the store's terms.

09

The app is provided as is. We do our best, but we cannot guarantee that every message arrives or that the app is free of defects.
